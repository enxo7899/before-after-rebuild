Our story

Gjon Gjoni opened the bakery in 1987 with one oven, a rented counter and a sack of flour his brother carried over from Lezhë. The oven is still the heart of the place.

Today his daughter Mira runs the front and the books, and his grandson Luka keeps the oven fire going before dawn. Drita, who joined in 1994, makes every tray of sweets by hand.

We use flour from mills in the Mat valley, butter from a dairy near Koplik, and honey from hives above Boga. We do not use improvers or pre-made dough.

At a glance

  • Wood-fired oven, lit at 4:30 each morning
  • Nine tables inside, four on the pavement in summer
  • Free Wi-Fi, high chairs and a step-free entrance
